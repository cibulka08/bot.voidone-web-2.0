What Void does

Auto-moderation removes spam and unwanted links. Warnings, timeouts and a full history keep your team informed.

Greet new members with your own message, give them a starter role and let them pick roles with buttons.

Members earn XP from chatting. Show rank cards, announce level-ups and hand out role rewards.

Keep a record of message edits and deletes, joins and leaves, and every moderator action.

Questions about Void.

What is Void?

Void is a Discord bot by Voidone. It handles moderation, welcome messages, levels and logging for your Discord server.

Who makes Void?

Void is made by Voidone, a small team that builds tools for Discord communities. You can find us on our support server.

What can the Void Discord bot do?

Void can moderate your server with auto-moderation, warnings and timeouts, greet new members and give them roles, track XP and levels, and log message edits, deletes, joins and moderator actions.

Can I add Void to my Discord server now?

Not yet. Void stays offline until the beta release, so the invite isn't available. Join the support server to hear the moment it opens.

Is Void free?

Void is free during the beta. Afterwards there will be a free tier and one paid plan. Details will be announced in the support server.

When does the Void beta start?

We'll announce the beta release in the support server. Join it to be among the first to add Void to your server.

What is planned for Void?

A visual command builder for your own custom commands and an embed designer for the messages Void sends are planned.

Is Void affiliated with Discord?

No. Void is an independent bot made by Voidone and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.

Where can I get help with Void?

In the support server on Discord, where the team and other server owners can answer your questions.

Can I join the Void team?

We're looking for developers, designers and moderators. See the open roles on our careers page.

Questions, bugs or ideas?

The support server is where we talk with beta testers and announce every update.
